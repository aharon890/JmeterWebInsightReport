# Exploratory testing log

What a human-like reader found by opening the report in a browser, and what happened to it.

**Why this file is tracked.** Exploratory testing is expensive and non-deterministic. Without a
record it produces the same discoveries every few months, each time from scratch, with nobody able
to say whether a thing was already seen and deliberately left. The *"not automated because…"*
lines are the valuable half: they are the accumulated reasons, and they are what stops the next
session re-litigating a decision.

**The contract** (`.claude/skills/explore-report/`): every finding ends as either a Playwright
spec or a line here saying why not. A session that found nothing still gets an entry — it records
that the mission was run.

**Confidence** is `verified` (seen repeatedly) or `unverified` (seen once, or seen over `http://`
on E19/E20 where the payload load path differs from `file://`).
